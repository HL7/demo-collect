ValueSet: AHIP Outreach Modes

Official URL: http://hl7.org/fhir/us/demographic-collect/ValueSet/ahip-outreach-mode Version: 0.1.0
Standards status: Trial-use Maturity Level: 1 Computable Name: AHIPOutreachMode

This value set includes content developed through the DEMo Initiative and is subject to joint copyright of America's Health Insurance Plans (AHIP) and Health Level Seven International (HL7).

Used as the answer options for the outreach preferences question in the Language Section, capturing a patient's preferred channels for health care outreach.
